Pickup address (private)

Only used in the messages you send to customers. It is never shown on the public site.

WhatsApp messages

You can use these words, which are filled in for each order: {name} {order_number} {pickup} {items} {address}. When you press Confirm, Mark ready, Mark collected or Cancel on an order, the status is saved first and then WhatsApp opens with the matching message for that customer. You can edit it in WhatsApp, and you tap send.

Add to home screen

iPhone (Safari): open this page in Safari, tap the Share button, choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android (Chrome): tap the three dots, choose Add to Home screen (or Install app), then Add.

It then opens like an app, full screen, as "Steam & Spice Admin". You stay logged in.

Backup and privacy

Export your orders as a CSV file every week (Orders, Export). The free plan has no automatic backups, so this file is your backup.

Customer names and phone numbers are personal data. Old customer details should be removed once the period you and the council decide has passed. A tool to remove details from old orders is planned; until then, do not share the exported files.
