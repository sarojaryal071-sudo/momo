# Brand direction — "Made together"

Status: v1, 2026-11-10 (decisions D-024, D-025). The brand name Steam & Spice is a placeholder. This document is the source for story, voice, copy and visual language. Inspiration is Dishoom's way of working (one story world, every detail serves it, real research behind it), never its assets, copy or layout.

## 1. The idea

Momo is often made together. Family and friends sit round one table and fold them side by side. Many people who love momo now live far from that table. The shop offers it back: homemade frozen momo you can steam at home, wherever home is now.

Two audiences, one feeling:
- Nepalis in the UK: a taste of home, recognised at once.
- Curious people: an easy, welcoming way into something new.

The site should feel professional, plain and beautiful: few elements, large type, generous space, one illustration language, short true words.

## 2. Real meaning: only verified facts, plus her own words

Dishoom's depth comes from research. Ours comes from two sources only: sourced cultural facts (below) and the owner's real answers (section 7). Nothing else is stated as fact.

| Allowed fact | Source |
|---|---|
| Momo is a dumpling central to Nepali cuisine: a thin wrapper, a filling, usually steamed. | [Smithsonian Magazine](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/travel/a-history-of-momo-the-dumpling-that-defines-nepali-cuisine-180988395/) |
| It is traditionally served with a spicy tomato-based dip, called achaar. | Smithsonian Magazine; [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Momo_(food)) |
| Making momo is often communal, with family and friends together. | Smithsonian Magazine |

Rules:
- **Never claim an origin.** The origin of momo is uncertain and disputed (first popular with the Newar of the Kathmandu Valley; historians disagree on the direction of travel). Do not write "invented in Nepal", "ancient", or "original".
- Any new cultural claim needs a reliable source added to the table above before it is used.
- Do not use these words unless the owner confirms them for her own product: authentic, traditional recipe, family recipe, secret recipe, original, best, award-winning, fresh or freshly made (the product is frozen), healthy, no preservatives, halal, vegan, gluten-free.
- "Hand-folded" and any claim about her process, who taught her, or where she learned must be confirmed first. Use a visible `data-todo` marker until then.

**Cultural care.** Do not use religious symbols (prayer flags, mantras, deities, Om) or the national flag as decoration. Keep to the motifs in section 5.

## 3. Voice

Simple and direct, warm underneath. Short sentences. UK English. Say what it is, what it costs, how to get it. Emotion comes from the hero line, the images and a few well-placed sentences, never from adjectives piled up.

- Good: "Homemade frozen momo, ready to steam at home."
- Bad: "Experience the authentic magic of the Himalayas."
- Use the word "chutney" (it is her word). Use "momo" without explanation on first mention only when the line follows with "dumpling".

## 5. Visual language

**Principle: restraint with one strong idea.** Each section says one thing. Large fluid type. Generous space. Colour comes in broad flat bands, not scattered accents.

- **Colour bands.** Full-width bands of charcoal, turmeric, chutney red and coriander on cream, with the existing palette only. Text and background pairs must pass the contrast rules.
- **The fold.** A hand-drawn, slightly imperfect pleat pattern, drawn as SVG, used as section divider and as a border for the chalkboard panel. It is the signature motif.
- **The ridge line.** One continuous hand-drawn line of mountains, as a single-stroke SVG across the hero and above the footer.
- **Steam.** Two or three small drifting wisps over the hero image or the steamer illustration.
- **Kraft tag.** A small label component echoing her sign (kraft paper, twine), used for prices, "Pickup only" and availability.
- **Chalkboard panel.** Keep it, with real text, as the menu.
- **Type.** Bigger and tighter display headings in a warm serif, plain body text. One self-hosted open-licence serif is allowed (D-025).
- **Images.** Few, large, warm and imperfect; honest alt text. Four slots until her real photos exist: hero, momo close-up, chutney bowl, kitchen corner. The layout must look good with placeholders and with real images.
- **Motion.** At most two subtle effects: slow steam drift and a gentle fade-in as sections appear. Content is fully visible without JavaScript and when reduced motion is requested.
- **Not allowed:** gradients, glows, emoji, rows of identical rounded cards, stock photos of people, decorative blobs, religious symbols, the national flag as ornament, and anything copied from another site.

## 6. Pages

Home follows section 4. Shop and how-to-cook keep their content and take the motifs. Cart and done stay calm and functional, with one motif at most.

## 7. Questions for the owner's friend (her real answers become the story)

1. Who taught you to make momo, and who do you fold with?
2. When is momo made or eaten in your home?
3. What do you remember about the first batch you made in the UK?
4. How would you describe your chutney in flavour words (not the recipe)?
5. What should someone feel when they open the bag?

Answers go into the About section and the chutney and hero copy, in her own words where possible. Until then, nothing personal is written.
