# SEO rulebook

Status: v1, 2026-11-10. Goal: Google can read everything that matters from plain HTML, and nothing goes public by accident.

## 1. Core rule

All indexable content exists in the HTML file as delivered. JavaScript enhances (cart buttons, pickup picker, notice banner) but never creates headings, product names, prices, or copy. Product data reaches the HTML at build time (see the build pipeline), not in the browser.

## 2. Launch gate

The site is **not indexable** until the owner decides (decision D-015). `SITE_INDEXABLE` defaults to anything but `true`. While it is not `true`, every public page gets `<meta name="robots" content="noindex">` and `robots.txt` disallows everything. Do not submit anything to Google Search Console before then. `SITE_INDEXABLE=true` also requires zero `TODO:` text and zero `data-placeholder` elements on indexable pages (the build fails otherwise).

## 3. Every public page needs

- A unique `<title>` of about 50 to 60 characters, with the page topic first and the brand last. Example: `Frozen homemade momo, pickup in the UK | Steam & Spice`.
- A unique meta description of about 140 to 160 characters, written for humans.
- A canonical URL built from `SITE_URL`.
- Exactly one `h1`, with `h2` and `h3` in order below it.
- Open Graph and Twitter tags: title, description, image (1200 by 630), URL, type.
- `<html lang="en-GB">`, viewport meta, and a favicon.
- Descriptive internal links ("Shop frozen momo", not "click here").
- Images with real `alt`, `width`, `height`, and lazy loading below the fold.

## 4. Structured data (JSON-LD, generated at build)

- **Organization** (or `FoodEstablishment` only if the owner agrees): name, URL, logo when it exists, telephone. **No street address** (home business). No `sameAs` until social accounts exist.
- **Product** for each product, with an **Offer**: `priceCurrency` GBP, `price`, `availability` (`InStock` or `OutOfStock` from the database), `url`.
- Never add ratings, reviews, `aggregateRating`, awards or hygiene ratings. Structured data must match visible content.
- Validate with Google's Rich Results Test and report the result.

## 5. Pages in and out of the index

- Indexable: home, shop, how to cook, and later allergens, privacy, terms.
- `noindex, follow`: cart, done, and anything admin. They are not in the sitemap and have no links from public pages except the cart link.
- `/admin/` has no links from the public site. Admin pages carry `noindex` and the sitemap omits them.
- Do not use `robots.txt` to hide pages that rely on `noindex` (crawlers must read the tag). `robots.txt` only blocks everything while the site is not indexable.

## 6. Sitemap and robots

`sitemap.xml` is generated by `build.mjs` from the list of indexable pages and `SITE_URL`, with `lastmod` from the build date. `robots.txt` points to the sitemap when the site is indexable.

## 7. Performance is SEO

Follow the performance budget in the technical rulebook. Core Web Vitals on mobile matter: fast hero image, no layout shift (explicit image sizes, reserve space for the notice banner), no render-blocking scripts (`defer` or `type="module"`).

## 8. Keywords and content

Natural phrases to work into headings and copy, never stuffed: homemade frozen momo UK, Nepali momo UK, frozen chicken momo, frozen veg momo, momo pickup. Write for the customer first. Do not copy competitor text.

## 9. Domain move

`github.io/momo` is a weak address for SEO. Buy a custom domain after the first version works (decision D-015). To move: set the custom domain in Pages, update `SITE_URL`, rebuild, and keep canonical tags pointing at the new domain. Because the old address cannot send redirects, add a short notice or meta refresh on the old site once the new one is live.

## 10. Local and later

Google Business Profile needs a service area or address decision with the owner. Not part of the first build. Revisit after council registration.
