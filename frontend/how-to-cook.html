From the freezer

How to cook frozen momo

Cook them straight from the freezer. Nothing fancy.

Steps

Steaming from frozen

  1. Take the momo you want to cook out of the freezer. Leave the rest frozen.
  2. Check they are hot all the way through before you serve them.
  3. Serve with chutney or hot sauce if you have it.
